Frequently Asked

The bits everyone asks

How much does a cleaning visit cost?

Most regular domestic visits start from around £18 per hour, with a typical visit lasting 2–3 hours. Deep cleans, end-of-tenancy and commercial work are priced based on the size and scope of the job. Get in touch for a free, no-obligation quote tailored to your home.

Are your cleaners vetted and insured?

Absolutely. Every cleaner is interviewed in person, reference-checked and DBS-screened before they step into your home. We carry £5 million public liability insurance, so you're covered on the rare occasion something goes amiss.

Will I have the same cleaner each visit?

Yes — that consistency is what makes the difference. You'll be matched with a regular cleaner who learns your preferences. If they're on holiday, we'll arrange a cover cleaner you've been introduced to.

Do I need to provide products and equipment?

It's your call. Many clients prefer us to use their own products; others ask us to bring everything, including hoovers and eco-friendly cleaning sprays. There's no extra charge either way.

What if I'm not happy with a clean?

We back every visit with our re-clean guarantee. Let us know within 24 hours and we'll return promptly to put anything right — no quibbles.

Not sure which service fits?

Give us a ring — we'll happily talk you through the options and quote on the spot.
