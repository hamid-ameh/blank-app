Our Story

Founded on a simple idea: cleaning should be brilliant, not bothersome.

Amvisphere Limited began with one founder, a clipboard and a long list of clients who'd grown weary of unreliable cleaners and impersonal agencies. We set out to do things properly — match every home with the right cleaner, pay our team a fair wage, and never settle for a cut-corners clean.

Years on, we've grown into a trusted name for hundreds of households and businesses across Britain. We've stayed small enough to know our clients' names, and large enough to cover holidays, last-minute moves and emergency call-outs without breaking a sweat.

The reason it works is rather old-fashioned: we care. About the homes we clean, the people who clean them, and the trust placed in us every time a key changes hands.
